## Automated license detection

GitHub may classify a custom license as unrecognized. Obsidian explains this warning in its [license-detection FAQ](https://docs.obsidian.md/community-directory/faq). Clarifying the terms does not turn them into a recognized standard license and does not guarantee catalogue approval.

A human reviewer should assess the existing license and private-source arrangement. The selected free-use terms have not been replaced with a different license to remove an automated warning.
